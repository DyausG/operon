### M1–M4: implementation

- **M1 work lifecycle** (`lifecycle.py`):
  - commands: `decline_work`, `reassign_work` (history kept through `supersedes_assignment_id`), a hardened `report_work`, the narrow `resume` from OBSERVING, and `cancel`/`escalate` that record open work;
  - `request_key` idempotent replay (checked before the revision CAS; same key with different content gives 409 CONFLICT);
  - `work_eligibility` (operon-work-eligibility-1);
  - `work[]` v2 with derived overdue flags;
  - migration `009_f1_2_plant_work.sql`: `simulator_asset_state`, unique report per assignment, unique actuation per report, an event index.
- **M2 verification** (`outcome.py`, `models.py`):
  - `operon-outcome-2`: `AWAITING_WORK` without writes; the plan is frozen only from the eligible report, with `observation_start` = the first second after the server-recorded report time;
  - the outcome binds the report;
  - D4: `LEGACY_RECEIPT_BOUND_PLAN` and `LEGACY_NO_WORK_ASSIGNMENT` are BLOCKED. They are derived on read, never written or evaluated; resume, escalate and cancel remain available;
  - projection `verification`.
- **M3 plant boundary** (`core/plant.py`, `simulator.py`, `engine.py`):
  - `PlantActuator`, with `SimulatorActuator` and `NullActuator` (production gives NOT_SUPPORTED);
  - every lifecycle-path simulator write goes through the port with a named cause;
  - dispatch and restart never actuate;
  - the eligible report is actuated synchronously (no tick in between), with an idempotent `PlantActuation` record;
  - failures are recorded as events and replayed at restart;
  - `SimulatorStateStore` gives durable plant state, with a pre-009 telemetry estimate for held assets;
  - projection `plant`.
- **M4** (`core/field_crew.py`, `engine.py`, `server/main.py`):
  - a deterministic SCENARIO crew, always on for the Guided Demo asset and opt-in otherwise with `OPERON_SIMULATED_FIELD_CREW`; never in production;
  - the Guided Demo completes through crew work facts;
  - routes: `/work/{id}/decline`, `/commands/reassign_work`, `request_key`, `asset_intervened`, `completed_instructions`; report provenance is SIMULATED outside production;
  - dispatch sends a canonical `alert` (phase `dispatched`) plus one `resolved`;
  - an `alert` (phase `verification`) on each sub-state change;
  - `business` counts verified outcomes only, with `events_dispatched` and `events_awaiting_verification`.
