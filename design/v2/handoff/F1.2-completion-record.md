## 1. Final architecture

**Invariant:** dispatch ≠ work performed ≠ plant response ≠ recovery ≠ verified recovery.

| Boundary | Durable fact | Writer | Code |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dispatch | `ExecutionReceipt` CONFIRMED, CMMS rows, `WorkAssignment` | executor / lifecycle | unchanged claim/receipt; the engine no longer touches the plant |
| Acceptance | `WORK_ACKNOWLEDGED` / `WORK_DECLINED` | assignee (`ActorRef`) | `LifecycleService.acknowledge_work` / `decline_work` |
| Work report | `WorkReport` + `WORK_REPORTED` (with eligibility) | assignee | `report_work`, `work_eligibility` (operon-work-eligibility-1) |
| Reassignment | new `WorkAssignment` (`supersedes_assignment_id`) + `WORK_REASSIGNED` | coordinator | `reassign_work` |
| Simulated execution | `PlantActuation` + `PLANT_ACTUATION_RECORDED` (or `PLANT_ACTUATION_FAILED`) | engine via `PlantActuator` | `core/plant.py` |
| Observation | `ObservationPlan` `operon-outcome-2` (binds the report) | verifier | `outcome.py` |
| Verified outcome | `Outcome` (binds the report) + phase change | verifier | `outcome.py` |

**Phases are unchanged.** OBSERVING carries derived sub-states:
- `work[].state`: ASSIGNED, ACKNOWLEDGED, DECLINED or REPORTED;
- `verification.state`: NOT_STARTED, AWAITING_WORK, WORK_NOT_PERFORMED, COLLECTING, EVALUATING, VERIFIED_RECOVERY, NOT_RECOVERED, REGRESSED, BLOCKED or ENDED_WITHOUT_OUTCOME.

**PlantActuator.**
- `SimulatorActuator` (sandbox and unspecified local mode) and `NullActuator` (production: NOT_SUPPORTED, nothing applied).
- Every lifecycle-path simulator write goes through the port, with a named cause: ANALYSIS_HOLD, HOLD_RELEASED, HOLD_REAPPLIED, RUN_TO_FAILURE, WORK_PERFORMED.
- Only `WORK_PERFORMED` from an eligible report changes the simulated plant towards recovery.
- The remaining direct simulator writes are the deprecated legacy demo (D10) and the Guided Demo scenario setup.

**Durable simulated plant** (`simulator_asset_state`, migration 009).
- Saved in the same commit as each tick's health scores. Work effects are saved before they are recorded.
- Restart restores the exact mode, progress, tick and profile.
- Active cases are re-held unless the plant is already responding to applied work.
- A pre-009 database estimates the held condition from persisted telemetry.

## 2. Work-report eligibility and verification policy

- **Eligibility** (`operon-work-eligibility-1`):
  - the current assignment of the executed dispatch;
  - acknowledged first;
  - COMPLETED or PARTIAL;
  - `asset_intervened=true`;
  - a bounded `performed_at`;
  - COMPLETED covers all instructions; PARTIAL names completed instructions and states the remaining work.

  Malformed or contradictory reports are refused and write nothing. NOT_PERFORMED and administrative-only reports are recorded but ineligible.
- **Verification** (`operon-outcome-2`):
  - `AWAITING_WORK` (no writes) until an eligible report exists;
  - the plan is frozen from that report, with `observation_start` = the first second after the server-recorded report time;
  - the thresholds are unchanged (3 to 12 post-work scores);
  - the report bounds the window and is never evidence.
- **Ordering:** the report and its plant effect run synchronously on the engine loop, with no tick in between.

## 3. Legacy cases and restart (D4, D1)

- **`LEGACY_RECEIPT_BOUND_PLAN`** (an `operon-outcome-1` plan without an outcome) and **`LEGACY_NO_WORK_ASSIGNMENT`** (dispatched before F1.1): BLOCKED.
  - Derived on read; never written, upgraded, evaluated or closed.
  - Work commands are refused with the reason.
  - `resume`, `escalate` and `cancel` remain available.
  - `reconciliation_required: true`.
  - A restart gives the same classification and writes nothing.
- **History:** v1 outcomes stay as recorded, labelled by their policy version.
- **Restart matrix** (each case tested):

  | Crash point | Result |
  |---|---|
  | During the adapter call | UNKNOWN, unchanged behaviour |
  | After the receipt | held, AWAITING_WORK |
  | After an in-tick hold, before the next save | re-held |
  | After acknowledgement | crew/assignee continue from durable state |
  | After the report, before actuation | replayed exactly once |
  | After actuation | the plant continues; never re-applied |
  | During observation | projection identical |
  | PERSISTS case | no longer flips to recovery |

## 4. API and WebSocket changes (as built)

See the contract document. In summary:
- **New routes:** `/work/{id}/decline` (sandbox gate) and `/commands/reassign_work`.
- **Report attestation fields:** `asset_intervened`, `completed_instructions`. Work about the simulated plant is SIMULATED outside production.
- **Retries:** `request_key` idempotent replay, reported as `replayed` in responses.
- **New projection fields:** `verification`, `plant`, and the `work[]` v2 fields.
- **Read model:** gains work and actuation arrays.
- **Dispatch messages:** a canonical `alert` (phase `dispatched`) plus one compatible `resolved`; F1.1 sent `resolved` twice.
- **Verification messages:** an `alert` (phase `verification`) on each sub-state change.
- **`business`:** counts verified outcomes only, plus `events_dispatched` and `events_awaiting_verification`.

## 5. Simulator and Guided Demo

- **Profiles** gain `partial_response` (default PERSISTS) and `field_response` (COMPLETES, PARTIAL, NOT_PERFORMED, DECLINES or NO_SHOW).
- **The deterministic `SimulatedFieldCrew`** (SCENARIO actor, SIMULATED work):
  - acts only through the ordinary work commands, with deterministic request keys;
  - always serves the Guided Demo asset, and every asset when `OPERON_SIMULATED_FIELD_CREW=1`;
  - never runs in production.

  Without a crew, cases truthfully wait in `AWAITING_WORK`.
- **The Guided Demo** keeps its status values. After approval, the crew acknowledges and reports, the simulated plant responds to the report, and verification closes from post-report scores.

## 7. Security and production limitations (unchanged or stricter than the baseline)

- **No authenticated identity exists until F3.** Production refuses every human command (`request_actor_kind`, `authorize`) and has no plant actuator and no crew.
- **Field-response routes** (acknowledge, decline, response) remain sandbox-gated. The legacy trusted flag applies outside production only.
- **`reassign_work`** follows the existing `/commands/*` gate: DECLARED actors in the unspecified local mode, refused in production.
- **Actors are not bound to assignees.** A declared or sandbox actor reporting work is labelled unauthenticated and recorded, not verified.
- **Unchanged from F1.1:** `/api/reset`, `/api/start`, `/api/stop`, `/execute` and `/outcome` still have no actor check. `/api/reset` is still an unauthenticated destructive endpoint.
- **Telemetry is still the simulator in every environment.** Production would have no real plant data or actuation; F2 adds the telemetry-source seam.
- **The F3 production blocker remains open. F1.2 is not production-ready.**

## 8. Remaining gaps, risks and deferred items

1. **V2 labels.** `frontend/src/v2/model/status.js` maps OBSERVING to "Verifying". Once integrated, that is inaccurate for `AWAITING_WORK` and `WORK_NOT_PERFORMED` until F4 consumes `verification.state` (contract §1). This is a cross-workstream dependency.
2. **No work-order recall.** Cancel and resume record that dispatched work orders are not recalled; OPERON has no CMMS cancel capability.
3. **Late field facts** (a report after the case left OBSERVING) are refused. They belong to the F3 work model.
4. **Overdue and stalled flags are derived only.** There is no automatic escalation (D9).
5. **The legacy demo path still couples approval to simulated recovery.** It is isolated and tested (D10), and documented here.
6. **Alert payloads grew.** They now carry work and actuation arrays; the F0 scale concern belongs to F8.
7. **Pre-existing flakes, reproduced on baseline:**
   - `test_configured_runtime_is_invoked_and_its_output_reaches_durable_artifacts`: real-time Guided Demo grounding;
   - the `make_fixtures.py` `investigating` capture race (F4-owned generator).
8. **The outcome policy's reason text** still says "post-intervention". The policy thresholds are unchanged, and the boundary is now post-work.
